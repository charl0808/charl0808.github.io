Privacy Policy — Rival Ships

The publishing tool behind the YouTube channel Rival Crafts (@BuildWarsBoat) and its Instagram account. Last updated: November 19, 2026.

In short: Rival Ships is a private tool used by one person, the channel owner, to make and publish videos on his own YouTube channel. It uses YouTube API Services. It reads the public comments posted on the channel's own videos to choose each day's most-liked suggestion, and it uploads the channel owner's own videos to his own channel after he approves each one. It does not sell, share or rent any data, it does not show ads, and nobody else signs in to it.

1. Who we are

Rival Ships is operated by the owner of the Rival Crafts YouTube channel for that channel only. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts. Contact: vinetcharles06@gmail.com

2. YouTube API Services

Rival Ships uses YouTube API Services. By watching or commenting on the channel's videos you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube data is handled by Google as described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. What information is used

  • Public comments on the channel's own videos, read through the YouTube Data API: the comment text, the commenter's public display name and the number of likes. They are used only to pick the day's most-liked suggestion, which becomes an upgrade shown in the next video, with the commenter's display name credited on screen.
  • The channel owner's own YouTube account, through Google sign-in (OAuth), only to upload his own videos to his own channel and to confirm which channel is connected.
  • Public statistics of the channel's own videos (views, likes, comment counts), shown to the channel owner on his own computer.

Rival Ships does not access private data of viewers or commenters, does not collect email addresses, does not track anyone, and does not combine YouTube data with other sources.

4. How comments are checked

Before a suggestion is used, it is filtered: comments with links, spam, insults, brands or copyrighted characters are never shown. An AI service (via OpenRouter) reads the comment text to help with that check and to write the narration. Comment text sent for this purpose is not stored by Rival Ships beyond the records below.

5. What is stored, where, and for how long

  • Everything stays on the channel owner's own computer. Nothing is uploaded to any other server except the video itself (to YouTube) and the short checks described above.
  • For each day's video it keeps: the chosen comment's text, the commenter's public display name and like count (they are part of the published video), and a short log.
  • Comments that were read but not chosen are not kept: they are discarded the same day.
  • The sign-in key for the owner's own YouTube channel is kept on his computer only. It can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Stored YouTube data is refreshed or deleted within 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services policies.

6. Sharing

No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and none is used for advertising. The only thing made public is the video itself, published on YouTube (and as a Reel on Instagram) after the channel owner approves it.

7. Your choices

  • If you do not want your comment to be considered, simply don't comment, or delete your comment on YouTube.
  • To ask for your display name or comment to be removed from our records, or for a video credit to be removed, email vinetcharles06@gmail.com. We act within 7 days.

8. Children

Rival Ships is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
